# Power-Up Poker Rules

1. Unless otherwise stated, the usual rules of Texas Hold'Em Poker apply.
2. A standard 52-card deck is used.
3. The cards are ranked from Ace (high) to 2 (low). An Ace can also be used
low in an A-2-3-4-5 straight or straight flush, which is five-high.
4. The game is played with 2 players.
5. Each player is dealt 2 hole cards face down, plus one power-up card face down.
6. The game consists of 2 rounds of betting: pre-flop and post-flop.
7. Each player can bet a maximum of 2 chips per round (check, bet one, or bet two).
8. Players can use their power-up card only post-flop, and it must be used at the same
time as confirming their bet, before they know the result of it.
If the hand ends before the flop, power-up use remains private, otherwise it is revealed
to all players what was used.
9. The 5 possible power-ups are as follows:
    1. Increase Rank. Increase the value of a card by +1. King goes to Ace. (5)
    2. Decrease Rank. Decrease the value of a card by -1. Ace goes to King. (5)
    3. Auto-ace. Upgrades rank of one hole card of a choice to Ace (same suit). (2)
    4. Pocket pair. Choose a hole card. Swap the other card with a card from the
    deck with the same value as the chosen card (but by necessity, different suit,
    first found). If no card of that rank remains in the deck, the power-up
    does nothing and you keep your original hand as-is. (2)
    5. Re-deal. Discard your current hole cards and draw 2 new ones from the top of
    the deck. (6)
10. The game ends after the post-flop betting round, as the turn and river happen
without player interaction, and the player with the best hand wins the pot.
Power-ups can create duplicate cards, making five of a kind possible. Five of a
kind ranks above a straight flush; a higher-ranked five of a kind wins.
11. The power-up deck contains 20 cards total cards.
12. Players start with 15 chips, and the game continues until one player has all the
chips.
13. There is an ante to encourage betting. Each player must contribute 1 chip to the pot
before the start of each hand. This means a maximum pot of 10 chips per hand
(2 antes + (2 players x 2 rounds x 2 max-chip bets)).
14. Due to some power-ups, five of a kind is possible, which beats any straight flush or royal flush.
